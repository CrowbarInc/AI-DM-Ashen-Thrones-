# AT-FG-05 — Evidence retention

Keep active campaign and investigation evidence, current regression baselines, unresolved
failure reproductions, governance records, completed milestone conclusions and meaningful design
decisions. Keep the governing test data and executable reproduction instructions with their
inputs. The campaign history index points to those records.

Superseded test output, repeat transcripts, duplicate logs and historical temporary workspaces
are eligible only after the essential outcome is preserved, any active reads are checked, and the
frozen exact inventory is reviewed. Keep ambiguous records until their provenance is established.
Any historical deletion requires separate explicit authorization of the frozen manifest digest.

Automatic cleanup applies only to newly created scratch positively owned by the creating process.
The process records its creation secret, verifies the exact owned workspace and parent, and
cleans up after validated publication or recorded abandonment. Keep pre-existing roots, parent
families and reparse points outside that operation.
