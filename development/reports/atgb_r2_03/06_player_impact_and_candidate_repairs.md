# 06 — Player Impact and Candidate Repairs

The repeated S02 sentence stalls information gathering despite progressively clearer questions. It makes a player repeat themselves and obscures the difference between an unknown fact and a misunderstood request. S02.3 additionally invalidates the player's correction by changing its subject. S05's wrong-person clarification broke continuity; that part is repaired. Its remaining uncertainty should concern which account/detail is intended, preserving player choice rather than selecting an unsupported topic. Rumor restatement must stay hearsay; board information must not be invented.

Observed recurrence is three S02 selections of related non-answers plus one S05 ambiguous follow-up. No frequency statistic or claim that most NPC conversations fail follows. Expected exposure extends to ordinary specific questions about broad public summaries, corrections with pronouns/negated alternatives, and follow-ups after failed or competing accounts. Those families arise naturally beyond these phrases, but future reliability is unmeasured.

Candidate 1 addresses the earliest demonstrated boundary without changing deterministic gameplay authority. It is a bounded **live interpretation dependency**, not a proven repair. Current instructions already forbid unsupported selection and require ambiguity handling; the evidence proves a contract-compliant but semantically poor completion, not that a particular missing sentence caused it. Proposed wording efficacy is a hypothesis requiring live acceptance. Do not expand the proposal into broad prompt optimization, new semantic classifiers or experimental integration.
