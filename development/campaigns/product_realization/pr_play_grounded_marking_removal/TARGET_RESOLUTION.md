# Target Resolution

Object resolution reuses the inventory identity check. A surface name, alias, or surface kind must match one accessible singleton with quantity 1 and an existing campaign identity. "My sleeve" and "the coat sleeve" resolve to the established sleeve. "The paper" resolves to the paper surface. "The cloth" resolves through the surface kind when that kind is unique. A missing object, a pronoun such as "there", two matching objects, or two accessible surfaces on one object stop the turn. Nothing is created and no identity is migrated.

Marking resolution uses only active markings whose visibility record is exactly `{"classification": "visible"}`. Concealed, restricted, legacy, and removed markings are not candidates, and their text is not used to decide the match. A distinctive word such as "warning" or "name" must occur in the marking text or in the recorded medium's name. A class-only phrase such as "the writing" matches every visible marking on that object. Zero matches leave the object unchanged. Two matches, including two copies of the same text, ask for one marking and remove none. The commit uses that marking's `modification_id`. The id is not written into the player sentence.
