# Remaining foundational limitations

- Current scene authority is scene-level. Exact orientation, distance, obstruction, and off-scene line of sight require explicit authored evidence.
- A missing observation supports uncertainty about presence. It does not prove that a subject is absent.
- Historical recall depends on a grounded reference and a retained, attributable observation, persisted discovery, or authorized dialogue exchange. Legacy rows without that provenance can yield uncertainty.
- Open-ended reference resolution remains at the interpretation or clarification boundary. The offline fixtures establish the tested host behavior and deterministic limits.
