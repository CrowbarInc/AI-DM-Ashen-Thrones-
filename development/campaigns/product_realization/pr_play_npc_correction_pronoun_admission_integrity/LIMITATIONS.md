# Limitations

Support is limited to a bounded initial sentence and supported question construction. Extra or uncertain
first-person clauses require clarification. TELL, free-text coreference, contrast-fragment corrections,
arbitrary descriptors and broader gestures remain outside this repair. A new explicit
question can take precedence over an earlier speaker but does not revise any committed action. The historical
capture does not establish a durable pending addressee clarification. Evidence keys have meaning only within the
current prompt. This evaluation replays the recorded output and measures no live provider behavior.
