# DISCOVERY VISIBILITY

The scene discoverable_clues catalog never crosses HTTP. Journal discovered_clues and ui.clues are sourced from the existing authoritative knowledge/publication owners. Newly selected information enters these views after deterministic resolution; repeats preserve known facts without adding a new discovery. Existing revealed_hidden_facts publication into journal known_facts remains with game.journal.

Only current/visited scene IDs populate the scene picker, replacing the complete future-scene catalog. Server-filtered affordances remain available for supported exits. World factions/projects/events lack a reliable item-level player-publication contract: omit them rather than guessing. NPC private role/trust/attitude are omitted. No new discovery or world authority was introduced.
