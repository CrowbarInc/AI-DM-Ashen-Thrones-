The published turns answer the action and keep the route. They do not propose a concrete next move in the prose. The UI suggested talking to the guard or the runner and examining the board again.

The engine fallback is accurate and dull. It repeats every visible fact. A player can tell that nothing new was found, but it does not feel like a spoken reply.

The wait is dominated by the provider. About 2.3 to 3.3 seconds is tolerable for this prototype and slow for a quick back-and-forth.

Overall provisional judgment: acceptable for a restricted investigation, not yet a good scene.
