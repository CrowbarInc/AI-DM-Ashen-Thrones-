# Paraphrase Controls

These are not hard-coded templates. The same resolver handles them.

- `Copy the warning onto my sleeve with the mud.` Succeeds when the coat, mud, and disclosed warning are established.
- `Use the charcoal to write the name on the paper.` Succeeds on the paper with the disclosed name, under PF1e, rules-light, and science-fiction extension records.
- `I jot the message onto the cloth.` Stays unresolved. No medium is named, and `cloth` is not an established surface alias.
- `I scratch the words into the metal.` Is recognized as destructive. It does not write, even if a metal plate is given an ordinary-writing surface.
- `I mark the location on my map.` Is recognized, then unresolved when no map exists.
- `I write down what happened.`, `I remember the warning.`, `I copy his fighting stance.`, `I make a mental note.`, `I tell him to write the warning.`, and a question do not become marking mutations.

No branch keys on the Frontier Gate scene, the patrol warning sentence, mud as a substance name, or a PF1e skill.
