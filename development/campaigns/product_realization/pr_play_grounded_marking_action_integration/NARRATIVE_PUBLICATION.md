# Narrative Publication

Committed success is sealed only after `save_character` returns, source `grounded_marking:committed` or `grounded_marking:replay`. Visible success: `You {verb} the known wording onto the {surface} with the {medium}. The marking is on it now. It reads: {content}`. Concealed success says the marking is concealed and does not say it is on it now. The actor's confirmation includes the text just committed. Ordinary observation still withholds a concealed mark.

Unresolved prerequisites start with `Nothing was written.` and name the missing facts: no established object, no usable medium, wording not established, more than one known wording, incompatible medium, no rules decision, unsafe consumption, or the character is not established as able. Destructive attempts: `Scratching or engraving is not carried out as ordinary writing.`

Conflict: `Nothing further was written.` Stale revision: `The object changed before the marking could be applied. Nothing was written.` Other technical failure: `The marking could not be confirmed. Nothing was written.`

Player text does not include request ids, receipt ids, or service names. The player HTTP inventory projection still omits markings. That limitation is unchanged.
