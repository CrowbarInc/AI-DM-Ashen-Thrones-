# TARGET REFERENCE CONTRACT

An exact current public target is projected from the resolver-selected interactable id and a full parsed player examination action. The action target must equal its authored label or alias. Hidden, invisible, locked or conditionally gated interactables fail closed. Existing exact public-observation label handling is retained for ordinary observation targets; no AI candidate contributes a name or authority. Current detached context is rebuilt per action; nothing is cached across scenes/sessions/campaigns.

Only finite complete attention sentences can bypass discovery-vocabulary overlap: examine/inspect/study/focus, pause/take a moment, turn attention, look closely/over, give attention, attention returns, another look. Full-string matching rejects appended clauses, invented adjectives and changed targets. An approved noun by itself never authorizes a claim. Genuine failed outcomes do not grant attention presentation; known/already-searched outcomes are separately classified even where the resolver uses success=False. Semantic/legacy gates still inspect every raw sentence and the final composition. Unknown or ambiguous assertions fall back.
