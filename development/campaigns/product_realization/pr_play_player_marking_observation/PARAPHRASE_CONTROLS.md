# Paraphrase Controls

Recognized and resolved on the controlled fixture:

- `I examine the writing on my sleeve.`
- `What does my coat say?`
- `I look over the markings on the cloth.`
- `Is the warning still written on my sleeve?`
- `I inspect the inscription on the paper.` The paper page has no marking, so the sentence names the page and does not quote the sleeve warning.

`Is the warning still written there?` asks which object is meant. It does not choose the sleeve. `I examine the writing on my collar.` finds no established surface.

These return no observation proposal:

- `I remember what the warning said.`
- `I tell him about the writing.`
- `I write another warning.`
- `I erase the writing.`
- `I inspect the patrol's behavior.`
- `I examine the road.`
- `I look at the gate.`
- `I read the notice.`

The HTTP negatives leave the character bytes unchanged and do not attach `marking_observation` to the turn.
