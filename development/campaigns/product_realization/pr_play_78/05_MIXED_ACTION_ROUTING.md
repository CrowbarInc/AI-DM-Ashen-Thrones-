# Mixed-action routing

Mixed requests are split on "and", "then", and a comma before a known verb. "Wait and watch" stays one step. A person-approach with no object is social, not a physical move of an object. Dialogue after a failed physical step is not attempted. Unsupported steps are stated.

- "Approach the guard and greet him." Moves beside the Gate Guard and speaks. Browser and live gate.
- "I thank the guard and step back." Speaks, then moves away. No new place is created.
- "Approach the guard and ask what concerns him." at the milestone, after scene scoping: the approach is unsupported and the question is not attempted.
- "I step closer to the notice board and look at the ground beside it." Unsupported. Nothing added.
- "Examine the notice board and ask the guard about the warning." in the HTTP campaign asserts a guard or notice response and forbids the northwest-mud sentence. The authored clue is not published on that mixed turn.
