# Player initiative

Ordinary unauthored requests that the existing owners can resolve are resolved. Examples that worked in play:

- Look around the gate and the milestone.
- Examine the notice board and the weathered milestone.
- Follow the missing patrol rumor to Old Milestone.
- Ask whether anyone passed, whether the guard needs help, and whether he seems nervous. Nervousness uses the established demeanor and says nothing establishes a hidden motive.
- "Am I standing?" answers from position and does not mutate.
- Wait and watch. Offer to help search. Look for a struggle. These return established uncertainty or an explicit refusal. They do not invent a search party, a clue, or a passage.

Unsupported, with the limitation stated and no success committed:

- "I climb the gate wall." / "I climb the milestone." No climbing check is configured. Position unchanged.
- "I climb the nearest wall." Feature not established.
- "I record the survey at the milestone." "That choice cannot be taken here now."
- "I return to report the uncertain trail to the watch." Treated as travel. The player stays. The suggested-action button with the same words does take the report branch.

"Who are you?" at a scene with more than one guard clarifies: "Which available person do you mean?" It does not pick a hidden identity.
