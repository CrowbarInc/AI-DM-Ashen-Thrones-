# Cross-domain continuity

Browser campaign A, one continuous session:

1. Look around (observation).
2. Examine the notice board (investigation, authored clue).
3. Step closer and look at the ground (unsupported physical/investigation, no mutation).
4. Approach and greet (movement plus dialogue).
5. Ask who passed (dialogue, bounded uncertainty).
6. Ask whether he seems nervous (same NPC, no hidden motive).
7. Snapshot, reload, resume.
8. Thank him and step back (dialogue plus movement).
9. Look for a struggle (investigation, nothing established).

The clue and the transcript were present after reload. The guard's goodbye landed after resume.
