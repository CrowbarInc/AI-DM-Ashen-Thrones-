# Limitations

Attention support is deliberately narrow. It covers the verified question forms and the canonical ASK operation. TELL, broader greeting or movement phrasing, and implicit social outcomes rely on existing admission. The scene’s sale-of-stew prose supplies no structured alias. Unsupported, ambiguous or contradictory descriptions can require a more specific addressee.

The standalone positive synthetic alias control binds the correct speaker. Its existing lexical topic extraction retains some alias wording and the receipt returns uncertainty. The named eye-contact controls pass the shelter question to the resolver as their dialogue subject.

The evaluation is offline. Historical replay verifies the entire captured prompt, restored state, and exact output text. Companion scenes and uncaptured global defaults come from the local repository. Three previous T16 posture controls now have different intended outcomes because both spans bind one present NPC. No measurement of live provider behavior was made.
