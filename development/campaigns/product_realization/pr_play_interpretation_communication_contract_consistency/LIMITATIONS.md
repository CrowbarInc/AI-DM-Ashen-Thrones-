# Limitations

Four captured live interpretations are exact offline inputs, not new model samples. Historical model scores remain unchanged (2 PASS / 2 PARTIAL); G02 and N2 were deterministic routing intercepts, never live-model cases. Related negative controls and wording variants were authored alongside the repair, not independently held-out generalization. No live narration quality, live latency, longer-play or model reliability improvement is established.

The reconciliation is deliberately narrow. Unknown wording, incomplete clauses and reference uncertainty may still require clarification. A4 answers only authoritative prepared spells, not an exhaustive possible-casting list or spellbook contents. B1 cannot establish the officer or soldiers' fate; no NPC dialogue subsystem was added. E02 still lacks broad environmental search resolution. Conditional/deferred execution, action planning, combat, inventory expansion and social-repeat work remain outside scope.

The known-content question repair covers complete “What does [object] say?” wording using existing visible evidence. Other legacy content-question forms retain their established behavior; this milestone does not redesign all information routing or narration. Existing inherited regression failures must remain visible by exact identity.

Session save metadata, request receipts and conversational/publication bookkeeping may change on read-only requests; gameplay-authoritative fields, character/resources, world, scene and combat must remain unchanged. Exact restored captured fixtures differ only in regenerated last_saved_at metadata. World tick measurements use a direct spy on api.advance_world_tick and are separate from historical derived measurements.
