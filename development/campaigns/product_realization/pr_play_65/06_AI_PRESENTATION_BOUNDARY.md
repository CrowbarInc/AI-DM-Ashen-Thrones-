# AI PRESENTATION BOUNDARY

Existing legacy mechanics, consequential safety and final validation remain required. Coverage is evaluated on the trusted segment and final response; only the AI segment's coverage obligation is empty. Authority remains unchanged. Optional prose must also fit a finite action introduction or an exact authorized observation/prior proposition. Unknown clauses fall back. This closes the offline missed-hidden/literal-claim cases without broad semantic expansion.

The finite boundary reduces stylistic freedom and rejects some harmless introductions. It is not proof of universal English safety. AI cannot establish new routes, items, NPC outcomes, travel, world changes or discoveries. Duplicated mandatory facts cause entire optional-segment omission, never trusted-text removal. Exact-authorized historical context is validated but not mandatory.
