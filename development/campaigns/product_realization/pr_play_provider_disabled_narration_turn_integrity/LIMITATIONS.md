# Limitations

No new gameplay capabilities. Withholding an unavailable request is not successful execution. An existing completed inspection still consumes time without inventing details; authored publication retains authority.

No actual model interpretation, unfamiliar-language generalization, live narration quality, provider retry/latency or real timeout reliability was measured. Implementation-authored variations are not independently held-out evidence. Historical semantic labels are preserved, not promoted into new success claims.

Legacy scene requests still have in-process same-key replay rather than a new durable HTTP receipt. A new request after restart can legitimately be another inspection. Mechanical actions separately have durable receipts, verified with empty lanes and fresh-interpreter reload. No universal transaction, response cache or multi-file crash-atomicity guarantee was introduced.

Legacy scene effects finalize after optional narration/recovery; this is distinct from durable mechanical commitment before publication. Other engine/storage failures and inherited regression defects remain outside this repair. Broad model understanding, sustained continuity, unsupported capabilities and social-repeat/legacy-stance behavior remain limited or deferred.
