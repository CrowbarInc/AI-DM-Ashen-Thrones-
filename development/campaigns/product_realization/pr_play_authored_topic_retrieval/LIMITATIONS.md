# Limitations

Provider-disabled results cannot establish live AI DM naturalness. Matching remains the existing bounded lexical subject/ranking mechanism; this work does not certify arbitrary paraphrases, every price/count form, unbounded comparisons or the separate migrated semantic-plan route. The comparison extension recognizes a finite comparative vocabulary and explicit than relations. Unknown information remains uncertainty, not successful retrieval or proof of absence.

Inherited regression failures are retained with exact identities. Performance has five samples per family under background load, so only descriptive medians/maxima are meaningful; no significance or production tail improvement is claimed. Integrity scope is explicitly enumerated in initial_integrity.json rather than claiming a whole-repository historical audit.

One indirect forbidden-password phrasing remains an inherited inspect misclassification with provider-disabled abstention. The matched expanded baseline proves the same classification, tick and safe non-disclosure before/after. This is not claimed as successful information retrieval or polished dialogue. The bounded repair does not change semantic admission.
