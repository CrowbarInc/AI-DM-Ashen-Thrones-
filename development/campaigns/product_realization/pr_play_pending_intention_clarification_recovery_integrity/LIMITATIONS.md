# Limitations

The live snapshots do not separately serialize the root campaign configuration or conditions file, or standalone
host logs beyond embedded host boundary traces and the saved transcript. Replay uses isolated defaults for those
documents and repository assets for inactive scenes. Captured effective scene, session, world, combat, character and
transcript are restored exactly. The baseline matches every captured provider input and publication. Wall-clock and
generated receipt IDs may vary. Repaired sequence prompts reflect the changed replies.

The authored examination reveals the missing patrol route. It supplies no authoritative curfew rules. Recovery is
limited to the verified affirmative reading and restatement frames. Arbitrary coreference and correction, unsupported
scenery, generic pending redesign and T11 remain outside scope. No live provider behavior was measured.
