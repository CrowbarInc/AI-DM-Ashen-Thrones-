turn; the final ledger observation says accepted_structured_opening and
gameplay_committed true. This establishes one successful sample only.

fallback presented as successful narration, campaign_started false, no committed
turn/time/faction progression. Budget refusal is HTTP 409 blocked.

STOP AND EXPORT AFTER SUCCESS AS WELL AS FAILURE. Also stop for generic
information fallback, exhausted budget, unexpected automatic retry, unresponsive
