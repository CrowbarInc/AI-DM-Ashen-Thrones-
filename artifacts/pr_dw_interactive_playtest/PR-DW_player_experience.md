PR-DW player experience

What worked:
- The player can load the UI.
- The current scene is understandable.
- Suggested actions are visible.
- The chat input is discoverable.
- The player can submit natural language.
- The GM response appears in the browser.
- The save summary updates.

Where it breaks:
- Ordinary OOC text can be interpreted as in-world inspection.
- The player cannot trust `OOC:` as a general non-mutating marker.
- The response to the generic OOC probe felt like a game response, not an OOC answer.
- Live narration generation can occur from a probe intended only to verify transport.

Player-experience conclusion:
- Browser integration is usable, but the game is not ready for unscripted live play because communication-mode safety is incomplete.
