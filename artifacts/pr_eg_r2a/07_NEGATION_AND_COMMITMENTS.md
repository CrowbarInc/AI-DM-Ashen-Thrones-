# 07 — Negation and Commitments

A refusal is a speech act, not a reward. “I can't promise ten silver” is safe here; “twenty crowns will be yours” and “I can owe you a favor” create expectations of resources/obligation. A conditional promise has a beneficiary, trigger and action; the condition does not erase the obligation. An explanation for refusal can invent an operational rule or resource condition.
