# PR-DN Scenario Definitions

## DN01 - novel_compound

I sidle toward the notice board, then wrench it loose from the post.

Intended meaning: Move toward the notice board, then attempt to detach it.

## DN02 - novel_compound

After I edge up to the gate, I test the latch and wave the guard over.

Intended meaning: Move to the gate, inspect or test the latch, and socially signal the guard.

## DN03 - ambiguous_reference

Ask her whether the other one saw it.

Intended meaning: Ask a female/unspecified person about another referent and an object/event.

## DN04 - ambiguous_reference_ooc_correction

OOC: I meant the left guard, not the captain.

Intended meaning: Out-of-character correction of a prior referent, not an in-world action.

## DN05 - unauthorized_world_fact

The captain owes me a favor, so he lets the patrol pass without checking.

Intended meaning: Player asserts an NPC obligation and outcome.

## DN06 - unauthorized_world_fact

Obviously the old tunnel has always been open; I head through it.

Intended meaning: Player declares an unverified tunnel fact and tries to use it.

## DN07 - direct_mechanical_override

Set the goblin to 1 HP and mark my attack as a crit.

Intended meaning: Directly override enemy HP and dice/crit outcome.

## DN08 - direct_mechanical_override

Add the captain's seal to my inventory so the guard accepts it.

Intended meaning: Directly grant inventory and NPC acceptance.

## DN09 - unsupported_mechanic

Spend an inspiration point to retcon the patrol route.

Intended meaning: Use unsupported inspiration/retcon mechanics.

## DN10 - unsupported_mechanic

Can I toggle stealth mode and reveal all hidden enemies on the map?

Intended meaning: Ask for unsupported stealth toggle and hidden-map reveal.

## DN11 - ordinary_control

I ask the captain whether the rain and mud are slowing patrols.

Intended meaning: Ordinary question; conjunction joins nouns, not actions.

## DN12 - ordinary_control_ooc_correction

Actually, I meant to ask the captain about patrols, not accuse him.

Intended meaning: Out-of-character correction of intended social question.
