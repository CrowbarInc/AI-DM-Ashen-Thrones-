# Findings and recommendations

## What was demonstrated

The quarantined hook can call the existing expression adapter after a real investigation resolution. Two live preludes were captured. They describe the notice board and the rain barrel in the visible scene. The discovery case does not add a second clue. The limited case does not reveal the patrol route. Player-facing text and authoritative state stayed on the baseline when narration failed or when the validator false-accepted a key. Usage accounting matches the two create calls. The focused regression gained 21 tests and no new failure.

## What remains unproven

The heuristic pass does not detect a found key, a named culprit, a confession, a failed check narrated as a success, or a shortened undisclosed route. Lexical overlap with the authorized sentence is not a semantic check. The rain-barrel sample also added "as the day wanes," which the validator accepted. Manual review called that unsupported atmosphere rather than a fabricated discovery. The next live sample could cross the line the validator misses.

The limited-investigation assembly still appends every public observation after the prelude. That was already true in PR-PLAY-52. It keeps the experimental text long even when the model is brief.

## What did not happen

Narration did not change the clue, the inventory, the conditions, or the player response. Hidden canaries did not enter the provider message. The live flag is not on for ordinary play.

## Verdict

**B — PARTIAL / FURTHER INVESTIGATION REQUIRED**

The live path is isolated and the two samples were usable under manual review. Semantic validation is still inconclusive, so this is not ready for a player-facing narration experiment.

## Primary next step

**B. Repair consequential-fabrication validation.**

That is the highest-value step because the quarantine can now obtain real prose, and the gate that would have to reject a bad sample still misses the cases this pass listed. Another live round would spend tokens without closing that gap. Player-facing narration should wait until those misses are either detected or explicitly left as known, tested limits.
