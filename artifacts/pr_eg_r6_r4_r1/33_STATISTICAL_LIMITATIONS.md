# 33 — Statistical Limitations

170 paired questions, 34 family clusters, 35 source records, 31 eligible records; 3 NPC profiles and 34 categories. Five questions per family are correlated; they are not 170 independent source situations. Holdout has 11 families/55 questions. Same author designed sources, labels and partitions; no independent human adjudication or blinding. Exact class, literal qualifier and short answer value judgments can be disputed. Synthetic prose, fixed assignment and single provider sample limit external validity. B/C separate extraction samples, different A output expressiveness, and history/question-type confounding limit causal conclusions. No question-level confidence interval is presented as independent-source evidence. Gold diagnostic is offline and exact-vocabulary only. Human review burden, semantic precision/recall and dollar cost remain unknown.
