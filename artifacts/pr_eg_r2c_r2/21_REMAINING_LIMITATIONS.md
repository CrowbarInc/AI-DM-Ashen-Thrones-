# 21 — Remaining Limitations

Grounding remains bounded to current scene entities, existing direct-address/conversation context, authored vocabulary and lexical topic selection. It does not invent missing referents or interpret arbitrary annotation prose. Unclassified unsupported annotations without provable grounding/inapplicability stay conservative. Known role identifiers are npc_to_ask/addressee/interlocutor; provider-authored arbitrary labels are not new authority.



Deictic subject handling is conservative. Arbitrary synonym/reasoning competence, richer hidden-knowledge access control, unlimited history, process-restart persistence and load performance are not established. Existing history remains capped at 32 rows and the 25 inherited failures remain out of scope. These boundaries do not justify unrestricted dialogue, new memory/authority systems or R3 implementation.
