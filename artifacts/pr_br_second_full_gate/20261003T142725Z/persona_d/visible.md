# Player-visible transcript

## Opening

Rain splashes over soot-dark stone as frayed banners flutter above the eastern gate of Cinderwatch, their once-vibrant colors dulled by ash and damp. Mud churns beneath a squeezed line of refugees and wagons, pressed tightly against the guards who maintain a strict choke. Nearby, a tavern runner calls out offers of hot stew and whispers of paid rumors, her voice a sharp contrast to the steady tension in the air. Along the crowded approach, a threadbare watcher stands motionless, eyes darting more to the faces and packs of those waiting than to the gate itself. Just beyond, a notice board catches your eye, its fresh postings listing new taxes and curfew rules, and most grimly, a warning about a missing patrol. A well-appointed townhouse, bearing noble colors, overlooks the muddy square with quiet detachment. The crowd’s murmur, the splatter of rain, and the watchful presence of guards mark the gate district as a place gripped by unease and watchful eyes.

"Such details aren't something I hear, and folks tend to keep their cards close around here. You might want to ask the gate serjeant; they keep the roster and might share more for the right price or favor."

## Turn 2

Player: I carry the stew to the gate serjeant, offer the bowl with a smile, and ask, "Who was on the missing patrol, and what favor would loosen the roster?"

"After the Ash Compact's census choke worsened, they redirected the patrol to avoid the eastern caravan bottleneck. As for the roster, those are held tight—only certain favors granted through official channels might see names loosened for public notice. If you want to pry that open, you’d need to speak with someone higher up in the guard captain's office."

"We watch movements, not always the shadows behind them. If you want names or leads, the guard captain’s office might share more—if you can persuade them. But beyond that, I’m afraid I can’t say."
