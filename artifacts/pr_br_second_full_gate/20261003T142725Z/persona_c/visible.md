## Opening

A steady rain spatters the soot-dark stone of Cinderwatch's eastern gate, where frayed banners flutter weakly in the chill wind. Refugees huddle in threadbare clothing, their faces drawn and anxious, as they wait among wagons stuck in the deep mud, hesitating at the gate's chokepoint tightly held by tired guards. Overhead, a well-kept townhouse flies vibrant noble colors, offering a stark contrast to the grimy gate square below. A notice board catches your eye, its worn wooden frame holding proclamations of new taxes and curfews, alongside a prominently posted warning about a patrol recently gone missing on the northwest trail. Nearby, a tavern runner calls out cheerfully, hawking hot stew and whispered rumors to the gathered crowd. One figure—distinguished by threadbare clothes yet oddly still—watches the cluster of refugees and travelers with a penetrating gaze, their eyes flicking not to the gate but to lists and faces beyond the gate line.
