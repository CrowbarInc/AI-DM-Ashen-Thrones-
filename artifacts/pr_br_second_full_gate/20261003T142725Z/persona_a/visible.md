## Opening

Cold rain spatters against the soot-dark stones that frame Cinderwatch's eastern gate, where frayed banners flutter in the damp breeze. The muddy approach is choked with refugees, wagons laboring through the mud, and foot traffic pressed tight beneath the watchful eyes of guards who hold the choke point firmly. A well-kept townhouse stands above the square, its noble colors a stark contrast to the grime and clamor below. Nearby, a notice board displays freshly posted taxes, strict curfew rules, and a warning about a patrol that has gone missing somewhere beyond the gate. Among the crowd, a figure in threadbare clothes watches with unnerving stillness, their gaze drifting more toward the packs and faces than the gate line itself. Near the rain barrel, a tavern runner calls out animatedly, offering hot stew for coin alongside whispered rumors, providing an undercurrent of restless chatter to the scene.
