# Player-visible transcript

## Opening

Rain spatters against the soot-dark stone of Cinderwatch's eastern gate, where frayed banners flutter reluctantly in the chill wind. Refugees huddle in threadbare cloaks, while wagons and foot traffic strain the muddy approach, pressing tightly against the guards who hold the narrow choke with weary discipline. A threadbare watcher stands apart, still and watchful, eyes flicking from packs to faces in the queue. Nearby, a tavern runner calls out warmly, hawking hot stew and whispered rumors to those waiting. A well-appointed townhouse overlooking the square bears noble colors, silently contrasting the day's muddy clamor below. A large notice board lists recently imposed taxes, curfew rules, and a stark warning concerning a missing patrol, drawing uneasy glances from the crowd.
