# 13 Candidate Source Selection

Candidate preparation uses the grounded speaker’s public or explicitly disclosure-permitted NPC-known records without lexical pruning. Provider context is capped at 12 sources, each at most 800 characters; oversized records are omitted rather than silently truncated. It sees no descriptors or values for denied records. Selection favors authored order; relevant records beyond the cap can be missed. Sources known to a genuinely unresolved addressee are not exposed.
