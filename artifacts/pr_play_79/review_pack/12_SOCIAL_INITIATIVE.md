# 12 — Social initiative

"I ask the guard to send someone with me" remains a decline: the engine does not deploy a patrol. "Hello, Gate Guard" is now a direct address because the name after the comma is the nominal. "Hello, captain" stays ambiguous, which is correct: no one there is named captain until the Thoran clue, and three people are present.

Social follow-up without repeating the name does not keep the guard. No persuasion check was invented and none succeeded.
