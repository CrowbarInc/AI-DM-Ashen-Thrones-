The road-name proposition permits “The street outside the gate is called the north road” and “Folks call it the north road”; it does not establish a fortress destination, the king as namer, road direction, safety or travel time. Existing source sentences remain propositions rather than automatically atomized truth graphs.

FRESH narrow validation rejects unsupported geography/history additions in the frozen cases, yet rejects six development paraphrases that preserve the road name. For example paraphrase01 and paraphrase02 are safe by the historical labels but rejected in batch. The fixed single-case paraphrase01 repeat is retained independently; no original result is overwritten. This demonstrates sensitivity to phrasing/batching, not a reliable semantic boundary.

Authorized destination and all-night gate-watch cases remain in the original corpus. Supplemental E explicitly authorizes operational security information and the current handler delivers that fact unchanged. This negative/positive distinction is essential: risk vocabulary cannot be a universal prohibition.
