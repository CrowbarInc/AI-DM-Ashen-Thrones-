# 10 — Speech Acts and Commitments

Answer, clarify and paraphrase operate over permitted propositions. Express preference requires incidental permission. Express uncertainty/refuse requires a posture consistent with actual knowledge. Asking a clarifying question is nonbinding only if its premises introduce no material facts. Negotiation may explore a request without offering a resource; an actual or conditional offer needs explicit resolver authority.

“I can’t promise you ten silver” negates an obligation. “I’ll pay you ten silver” establishes one. “I’ll pay if my captain approves” offers conditional payment without an authorized obligation. “I can’t pay without my captain’s approval” invents an organizational rule unless supplied as evidence. The speech-act label is not enough to distinguish them.
