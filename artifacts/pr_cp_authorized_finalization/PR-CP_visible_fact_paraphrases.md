# PR-CP visible fact paraphrases

## Contract

Describing a fact already on the scene or in the journal is not a discovery. The frontier gate already states: "A notice board lists new taxes, curfews, and a posted warning about a missing patrol."

The player may be told that sentence. A paraphrase may add at most one content word under `_empty_identity_covers`. Physical manipulation, hidden text, invented inscriptions, roster names, dates, and orders are outside this contract.

The board does not need an interactable object definition for that already-visible sentence to be narrated. `authorized_observation_line` exists for this case.

## Conflict that was not widened

The PR-CO paraphrase ("edicts", "urgent", "freshly posted") adds more than one content word. That cover was not loosened. Those words do not reach the player.

`notice_roster_history` also matched the exact authorized sentence, so the observation helper refused to emit it. The repair licenses the pattern when the proposition restates the authorized fact and adds no content word. The exact sentence now seals. A failed loose paraphrase is replaced by the exact visible facts: taxes, curfews, and the missing-patrol warning.

## Still denied

Twelve named watchmen, a date of yesterday, an order to search the east road, annotations, and any wording that is not the visible fact. Hidden content is not authorized. Examining the board does not become a new discovery record.
