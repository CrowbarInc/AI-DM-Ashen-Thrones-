## Opening

Cold rain spatters against soot-dark stone above Cinderwatch's eastern gate, where frayed banners snap in the gusty wind. The muddy approach is clogged with refugees and wagons, their wheels sinking into the slush as guards hold a firm choke point amid the pressing crowd. Nearby, a notice board stands heavily weathered but clearly visible, its posted lists detailing new taxes, curfew rules, and a stark warning about a patrol that has gone missing. A tavern runner calls out sharp offers of hot stew and paid rumors, weaving deftly through the throng. Against this restless backdrop, a figure in threadbare clothes stands motionless, eyes flicking cautiously toward looming packs and faces rather than the gate queue, as if waiting for something unseen.
