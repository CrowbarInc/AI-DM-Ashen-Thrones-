# 11 HISTORICAL RECALL AND COMMITMENTS

Commitments with a proposition still need a permitted source. A proposition that is only a preference is bounded and may be reused ([game/dialogue_authority.py:316](../../game/dialogue_authority.py#L316)). A commitment whose text is protected is not reusable. Speaker identity on the delivery row is unchanged. Recall quotes the stored line; it does not invent a new fact.
