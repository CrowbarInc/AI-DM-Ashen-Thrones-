# 03 REGRESSION FAILURE CLASSIFICATION

Every preserved new identity was classified before production edits. The first failing decision was `seal_host_speech` or `historical_disclosure_permitted`, not a later narrator.

- World-source fixtures put an authorized fact on `topic_revealed` without an NPC receipt. The seal treated the missing receipt as denial and replaced the fact with a refusal. The original expectation, that the authorized fact is delivered, remains valid. The helpers now bind a public NPC source and the host receipt that production social resolution already writes. Assertions were not rewritten.
- Historical and continuity rows were rejected because a delivered proposition was promoted back into a world-source check, or because a legacy row had no digest. Expected recall of non-protected delivered speech was still valid.
- The morning preference commitment had no `disclosure_scope`. It is bounded expression, not a secret.
- `test_provider_evidence_cannot_recall_fact_for_incompatible_question` expected uncertainty (`couldn't`) and received a disclosure refusal because the prior proposition vetoed the whole line.

No new identity was an actual unauthorized disclosure. Identities are grouped in `regression_comparison.json`.
