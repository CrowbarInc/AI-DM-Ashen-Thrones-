# 06 WORLD SOURCE AUTHORITY

Consequential disclosure still requires `authorize_npc_disclosure`: a matching NPC source, known status, and a public/allowed/authorized policy. Unknown policy and unknown knowledge fail closed. When world is present, a caller-supplied receipt is ignored and the NPC source is resolved again. A pure caller without world must present a host receipt whose validation, provenance, speaker, and proposition match, and that receipt cannot authorize protected wording.
