## Opening

Rain spatters against soot-dark stone beneath frayed banners fluttering above Cinderwatch's eastern gate. The muddy approach is clogged with refugees, wagons, and travelers, shuffling forward under the watchful eyes of guards holding a tight choke. A ragged figure in threadbare clothes stands motionless at the edge of the crowd, their gaze flicking from packs to faces more keenly than the line itself. Nearby, a tavern runner calls out above the murmur, hawking hot stew and whispered rumors for coin. Against the backdrop of damp grit and restless movement, a notice board displays fresh postings: new taxes, curfew rules, and a stark warning about a missing patrol. The scene is thick with guarded tension, authority's quiet pressure pressing upon the gathered masses at the gate.

## Turn 2

After that, I will approach the tavern runner and ask if they've heard any rumors about where travelers might find shelter or food nearby.

"From what I've heard," the tavern runner says, glancing toward the muddy crates nearby, "the patrol vanished somewhere near those muddy footprints to the northwest, just past the crates. No one’s seen them since." They lower their voice slightly. "Places like that aren’t safe for travelers, but you might find shelter further into the city or perhaps ask the guards at the old milestone. Food and a roof over your head come with a price, though."

## Turn 3

Player: I thank the tavern runner for the information and ask, "Are the guards at the old milestone known to be helpful, or do they expect payment as well? Also, is the city harbor a safer place to weather this storm?

"I can't say for sure whether the guards at the old milestone expect payment or are helpful," the tavern runner replies cautiously, wiping rain from their brow. "Rumor has it that place is less welcoming these days, so it might be best to proceed carefully if you head that way. As for the city harbor, it's said to be busier and better guarded during storms, which could make it safer for shelter, but that also means crowds and trouble can gather there. You might want to check with the guard captain inside the city for more detailed advice."
