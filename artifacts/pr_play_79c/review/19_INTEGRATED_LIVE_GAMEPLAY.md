# Integrated live gameplay

The HTTP campaign is the integrated session. It has 17 scripted gameplay turns plus the opening and one resume turn, 19 transcript rows in all.

It includes the seven-line conversation, environmental looking, approach, step-back, investigation, the milestone discovery, the grounded no-op, save, load, and resume.

Player inputs are the developer acceptance script recorded in `machine/http_transcript.json`. They are not an unguided person's improvisation.

Deterministic fallback on the verbatim eighth dialogue turn is not counted as live output.
