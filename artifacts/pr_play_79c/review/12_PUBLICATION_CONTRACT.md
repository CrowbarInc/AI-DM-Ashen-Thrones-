# Publication contract

The contract values are unchanged: FINAL_ENGINE_RESPONSE, ADJUDICATION_RESULT, LIVE_EXPRESSION_REQUESTED, CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED, UNSUPPORTED_ACTION, FAILURE.

Dialogue turns are FINAL_ENGINE_RESPONSE and skip primary narration. Physical mutation remains eligible for one bounded expression. A rejected expression keeps the mechanical sentence.

`last_publication` records the owner, whether dialogue was accepted and published, and whether narration was requested. The conversation owner and the integrated owner both write this stamp.
