# 08 — GM Recall

GM historical recall uses actual delivered NPC records and preserves original speaker and spoken content: `Mara said: "..."`. It never reports the speech as established world truth. Explicit subject/speaker constraints precede recency. Long and uncertain statements remain intact; a missing speech referent receives a historical clarification. Other GM explanation/public-evidence questions continue through their existing path.

Final primary turn 14 and secondary turn 12 select the earlier road statement after preferences, uncertainty and refusals. live_semantic_audit.json checks the exact prior delivered record and GM ownership. Deterministic owner controls separately test speaker ambiguity, absent NPCs, missing referents and lack of canonical authority from beliefs.
