# 07 — Referent Lifecycle

Lifecycle uses three axes: answer_status=direct/partial/unknown/refusal/clarification; focus_status=active/superseded/archived; correction_status=original/revision/corrected. Accepted sealed new question creates active/original. New focus supersedes prior active. Correction creates active/revision with incremented revision and marks parent corrected/superseded. Ordinary follow-up creates archived/original linked to its proposition while retaining active original focus. Rejected interpretations do not transition referents. Retention removes old records beyond the cap; their stable IDs can remain in bounded lineage links but unavailable records cannot be resolved. Temporal follow-up focus and multiple pending clarifications need stronger lifecycle semantics before acceptance.
